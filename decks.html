CURRENT DARK BANDIT DECKS

Three current decks built around Dark Prince and Bandit, representing different ways to create pressure, manage Elixir and adapt to the opponent.

01 · CURRENT DECK

LOGBAIT

Bait · Bridge Spam · Pressure

A combination of Log Bait and Bridge Spam that aims to force awkward defensive choices while dealing steady, inexpensive damage.

KEY STRATEGIES

  • Bait key spells and defensive counters before committing your most important threats.
  • Use bridge pressure to punish opponents who spend too much Elixir answering the bait.
  • Look for repeated, inexpensive tower damage rather than relying on one large push.
  • Adapt between bait pressure and direct Bridge Spam depending on the opponent's cycle.
KEY PRINCIPLE Force awkward defenses, then punish the gaps they create.
02 · CURRENT DECK

3 MUSKETEER BRIDGE SPAM

Split Pressure · Elixir Management

A demanding Bridge Spam deck where Elixir management, spell baiting and flexible use of Three Musketeers are central to maintaining pressure.

KEY STRATEGIES

  • Manage Elixir carefully and know when preserving an advantage matters more than immediate pressure.
  • Time the Elixir Collector according to the opponent's pressure and available answers.
  • Bait important spells before committing vulnerable or high-value troops.
  • Use Three Musketeers for distraction, DPS or split-lane pressure depending on the situation.
  • Against heavier decks, use Bandit and Dark Prince to maintain pressure in both lanes.
  • Adapt the role of the Three Musketeers to the matchup rather than using them the same way every game.
KEY PRINCIPLE Manage your Elixir, manipulate their spells, then choose how your Three Musketeers create pressure.
03 · CURRENT DECK

P.E.K.K.A BRIDGE SPAM

Defense · Counterpush · Pressure

A heavier Bridge Spam approach built around P.E.K.K.A's defensive strength and the fast pressure of Dark Prince and Bandit.

KEY STRATEGIES

  • Use P.E.K.K.A primarily as a defensive resource and avoid spending it without a clear purpose.
  • Turn successful defenses into counterpushes with the surviving troops.
  • Use Bandit and Dark Prince to add fast pressure around or after defensive plays.
  • Punish opponents who overcommit or invest heavily in one lane.
  • Adapt the amount of bridge pressure to the opponent's cycle and available counters.
KEY PRINCIPLE Defend efficiently, then turn the defense into pressure.

Deck lists and strategy can evolve with balance changes and the wider game meta.
