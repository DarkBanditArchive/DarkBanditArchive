FANFICTIONS

DIFFERENT INTERPRETATIONS

Fanfiction gives the pairing many different forms. Rather than attempting to catalogue every story, the archive highlights recurring interpretations of Dark Prince and Bandit's relationship.

ENEMIES TO LOVERS

They begin on opposite sides, with rivalry and distrust gradually developing into affection and mutual respect.

PARTNERS IN CRIME

Two outlaws working side by side, sharing adventures, danger and the kind of partnership that can become something more.

LONG-BUILD CHEMISTRY

Their relationship develops slowly through repeated encounters, teamwork and an increasing understanding of one another.
