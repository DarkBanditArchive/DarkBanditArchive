ABOUT THE ARCHIVE

Dark Bandit Archive is an independent fan-made archive dedicated to documenting the history, official appearances, fanworks and community surrounding Dark Prince × Bandit. The archive also preserves selected fanworks and information about other ships involving the Dark Prince.

Independent Archive

Dark Bandit Archive is created and maintained by fans. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or officially connected to Supercell.

Fanworks & Artist Credit

Fanworks remain the property of their respective creators. The archive does not claim ownership of fan-created artwork, writing, edits or other works. Artists and creators are credited whenever their work is documented here.

Official Material

Clash Royale, Dark Prince, Bandit and related characters, artwork and assets belong to their respective rights holders. Official material is documented here for archival, informational and fan community purposes.

Archive Philosophy

The archive aims to distinguish official material from community interpretation. Historical claims are presented as documented discoveries where possible, while fan interpretations are treated as part of the fandom rather than as canon.

Document, Don't Declare. Dark Bandit Archive preserves what the fandom has created and documented without speaking for the entire community.
