CANON & OFFICIAL

The cards, gameplay, official material and alternate stories that document Dark Prince and Bandit across the Clash universe.

THE TWO CARDS
GAMEPLAY

DARK PRINCE & BANDIT

Their connection begins with the two cards themselves. Dark Prince and Bandit each bring a distinct form of pressure to the Arena, and their abilities naturally complement one another in aggressive decks.

DARK PRINCE

Dark Prince is a melee troop with a shield, area damage and a charge. His splash damage makes him effective against groups of weaker troops, while his charge allows him to deal increased damage after building up speed.

BANDIT

Bandit is a fast melee troop whose dash lets her close the distance to a target and deal increased damage. Her speed and ability to cross the Arena quickly make her a strong pressure tool.

THE CLASSIC DUO
BRIDGE SPAM

HOW THEY ARE PLAYED TOGETHER

Dark Prince and Bandit are a classic Bridge Spam pairing. Dark Prince provides durability, splash damage and charge pressure, while Bandit adds speed and burst damage through her dash. Together, they can force an opponent to react quickly and spend Elixir defending the bridge.

GAMEPLAY TIPS

PRESSURE TOGETHER

Playing both cards together creates two different threats at once: Dark Prince's splash and charge pressure combined with Bandit's dash.

LET DARK PRINCE LEAD

Dark Prince can absorb pressure and deal with groups of troops while Bandit follows up or takes advantage of an opening.

PUNISH OVERCOMMITMENT

If the opponent spends too much Elixir defending one lane, use the other card to create pressure on the opposite side.

SUPPORT THEM

Neither card can target air, so the rest of the deck should provide reliable answers to air troops and defensive buildings.

DID YOU KNOW?

A Dark Bandit Cart Log Bait deck appeared as early as 2020 — and was still reaching the Top 200 in mid-2026.

BATTLE TIP

Play Dark Prince and Bandit in the same lane to combine their damage, splash, and charge abilities.

OUTLAWS & PARTNERS IN CRIME
OFFICIAL PROMOTIONAL MATERIAL

THE WANTED DUO

In the November 2024 season, Dark Prince and Bandit were presented together as outlaws and partners in crime. Their wanted imagery places them within the same western setting and gives their connection a very different interpretation from their usual Arena appearances.

WANTED OUTLAWS

Dark Prince and Bandit are presented together as wanted outlaws and partners in crime.

READY FOR THE FIGHT

Dark Prince can be seen standing in the back of the loading screen, ready to attack Evolved Musketeer and Cannon.

The scene also connects to Bandit's wider Forest Gang setting. The Rascals are part of that gang, placing them within the same conflict, while Dark Prince is depicted alongside them as another wanted outlaw.

ANOTHER UNIVERSE
OFFICIAL MANGA

ON OPPOSITE SIDES

The official manga offers an alternate interpretation of the Clash characters. In this universe, Dark Prince and Bandit find themselves on opposite sides of the conflict.

This story stands apart from the main Clash Royale setting, showing that official material can explore different versions of the characters and their relationships.

BATTLE TIP

Send them down opposite lanes to keep the pressure on — and overwhelm your opponent.

OTHER DARK PRINCE SHIPS

Dark Prince is shipped with a variety of characters across the Clash Universe, not only within Clash Royale. From fellow Royals and warriors to characters from other Clash games, fans have explored many different dynamics around the Dark Prince.

This section highlights a few of them, preserving fanworks and celebrating the different interpretations that have grown around the character.

DARKBOSS

DARK PRINCE × BOSS BANDIT

Two strong-willed figures with very different worlds behind them. DarkBoss explores the chemistry between Dark Prince and Boss Bandit, bringing together a disciplined royal warrior and a formidable outlaw leader whose confidence and independence make her a natural equal to him.

BUCKETWING

DARK PRINCE × BATTLE HEALER

A contrast between the hardened Dark Prince and the compassionate Battle Healer. BucketWing plays with the balance between strength and care, placing a stoic warrior beside someone whose role is to protect, restore and support those around her.
